Business Rules
Email and mobile number must be unique.
Passwords must always be stored as hashed values.
Inactive customers cannot log in or place new orders.
Deleting a customer should not remove completed orders; order history must remain for financial records.
Export should include only the currently filtered customer records.
Status changes, profile edits, and deletions must be recorded in the audit log.
Customers with active shipments or pending refunds should not be permanently deleted; instead, mark them as inactive.

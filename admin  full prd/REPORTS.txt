Business Rules
All charts respect the selected Year and Month filters.
Revenue is calculated only from completed/paid orders.
Average Order Value = Revenue ÷ Total Orders.
Cancelled orders are excluded from revenue calculations.
Coupon analytics include only successfully applied coupons.
Shipping analysis includes only shipped or delivered orders.
Export downloads the filtered report data only.
If no data exists for the selected period, charts should display an empty state instead of errors.
